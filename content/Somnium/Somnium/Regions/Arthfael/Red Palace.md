The Red Palace is the best place in the world to study magic, it is an honour to be granted the possibility to study here with some of the best Mages worldwide.

There are 2 main Branches inside the Red Palace:

1. Combat: This is by far the largest Branch in the Red Palace. Here promising Mages get trained on how to most effectively use their given powers as well as find suiting partners for them that complement each other's abilities. Another aspect is to strengthen connection through the Other Realm. The training is very hard and for about a third of all students, it live changing or even fatal. The training ranges from just some cardio training to simulated wars and missions behind enemy borders. But if a student survives until the end and passes their final exam they can proudly call themselves a member of one of the scarlet orders.

2. Research: This branch consists of a collaboration with the sultanate and its Ivory Temple, in these 2 institutions the smartest people of the world gather to examine old scriptures and artefacts that were found inside ruins all over the world and try to figure out the secrets of the world and why these sometimes more advanced civilizations vanished from it. They also try find new and better cultivating technics to strenthen the connection to the Realm of the Gods.
