The tribe union of the Great Akim Groves is a collection of the native tribes that inhabit the Great Akim Groves and its surrounding land. The union formed when the old king of Vonnegut started hunting the citizens of the Great Akim Groves because the vast majority of the people that inhabit this part of the world are animal-like humanoids, and the old king saw them as inferior and did not recognize them as humans, they could be slaughtered without a second thought. In general, these tribes are led by 1 Person who-through some kind of trial gained the trust of his people. If the Forest or its inhabitants face great danger in any way, shape, or form, they swore to unite under the union's banner to prevent further damage. They are friendly towards the sultanate because when the old king of Vonnegut decided to invade the forest. The Sultanate sent troops to help push back the invaders. To all other factions, they are neutral but want to be left alone for the most part. Their society is mostly based on small settlements with little tech inside the forest encampments, but in the greater city outside the forest technology is used more frequently, and the citizens will try to invent new technologies that combine their rituals that are conducted in nature with the resources and technologies which they trade from the sultanate.
