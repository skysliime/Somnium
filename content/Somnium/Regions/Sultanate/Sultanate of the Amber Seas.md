The sultanate of the amber seas is ruled over by 3 families that go back to the creation of this nation. They once arrived in this barren wasteland and discovered something in a ginormous ruin that helped them to create this thriving metropolis which is the Sultanate. no one knows exactly what they found back then in the depths of the ruins that are now entombed under the capital, but it had to be powerful enough to raise up a ginormous city out of the desert sand.

### The Zuhayr:

Are the smallest of the 3 Families, but have amassed the most power of all of them. This is because they handle affairs with other nations as well as the politics inside the nation. It is rumoured that the Zuhayr know some kind of mind-altering magic, but there is no hard evidence to support this besides their effectiveness in matters of politics

### The Aretas:

Are the second largest of the 3 Families, they specialize in mining operations but also manage the factory's and the food supply. they are easily distinguished by their deep green eyes that look like emeralds in combination with a slender and short frame. they posses some kind of magic that helps them to find large mineral deposits, or at least that is what everyone thinks their magic does, although the Aretas never stated this themselves.

### The Gadis:

Are by far the largest of the 3 Families, they specialize in military prowess and exploration. In short they can be summarized as adventurers, members of this family Posess a whole bunch of abilities and skills which helps them overcome the problems they could face while exploring one of the multiple ruins buried in the sands around the city. the main bloodline of this family is distinguished by their eyes, there is no single color or shape that is passed down to each new Generation but all of the Gadis have a colored Sclera and their Iris looks like a multitude of geometrical shapes.
