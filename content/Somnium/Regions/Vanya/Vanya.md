Vanya is the smallest Nation across the known world. Its army is small compared to the other nations but their Mages are more skilled than every other nations except Arthfaels. In the past war, many nations tried to conquer Vanya, but all of them failed because of the 2 Towers of Iknur that struck every vessel that tried to cross the open sea with a concentrated beam of light. In the past, there were once 3 towers but the previous ruler of Vanya tried to mount one of these towers on a ginormous ship so that he could potentially use it to wage war. The day on which he planned to move the tower onto the ship is known as "new Dawn" because that night the Sun could be seen emanating from the island where the third tower, as well as the ship, were located at. This light could be seen all over the continent and it seemed as if the night had ended and the sun already started to dawn on the horizon. The aftermath of this was that the island on which the third tower was located vanished as well as the old King and his ship. The Citizens of Vanya took this as the wrath of Iknur (the god of the Sun) and so vowed to never try to touch her creations ever again. This Nation is ruled by the Amri who get chosen via a tournament that is held every 10 Years. Only citizens that were born in Vanya are allowed to participate, as well as know what skills will be tested.
