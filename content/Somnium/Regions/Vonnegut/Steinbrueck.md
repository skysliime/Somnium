Steinbrueck is a small town in Vonnegut about 60km east of the border of Arthfael.

This small village was booming with life in the past because of the silver mines underneath the town. But once the mines ran dry the small town slowly deteriorated to its present state. Now Steinbrueck is just another village that keeps itself afloat by selling trinkets made out of silver scraps and lumber gathered from the forest nearby and tours through the mines and old factory buildings.
